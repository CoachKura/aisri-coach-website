## Publishing checklist (from the copyright review)

1. Apply the fixes in `reviews/copyright.json` and replace the `build.js` trademark and credits paragraph as above (the Tamil `strings.json` needs the same change).
2. Confirm the Sanjivani Jadhav dates against the AIU decision, and re-check every doping sentence against the primary notice (AIU/USADA).
3. For every direct quote of a real person, record in a quote log: the exact wording, the primary outlet, the date and the URL. Where a quote reaches us through a secondary source (shyamgopan.com quoting Mid-Day; Olympics.com quoting Inspire Institute; Rediff quoting ANI), try to cite the primary source.
4. Keep quotes short (under about 40 words each) and never reproduce news paragraphs. No permissions are needed at the current lengths. **Permission is needed** if you add longer extracts, any photographs, logos, race posters or screenshots (Garmin/Strava screens, VDOT calculator output), or any table copied from Daniels' Running Formula or the ACSM position stand.
5. Optional courtesy step, recommended for a book that profiles living athletes: send the named living athletes (or their federation or media contact) the passages about them, especially those covering doping, injuries, family deaths, housing or employment. Remove private family names unless they are needed.
6. Do not use athletes' names or likenesses on the cover, blurb or ads without written consent. Doing so implies endorsement and raises personality/publicity-rights risk in India.
7. Do not use "Born to Run", McDougall or the book's cover style in marketing. Keep AKURA's own title.
8. Trademarks: check that "AKURA Endurance Index" does not conflict with existing registered marks before using ™ commercially, and consider filing in India (Class 41/42/9).
9. Keep the "not medical advice" and "not validated" notices on the copyright page and in Appendix A (both are present).
10. Keep `research/` dossiers and the URL lists as the evidence file behind every factual claim about real people.

Full findings with evidence are in `source/reviews/` (one JSON + summary per team, plus `_applied_log.json`).

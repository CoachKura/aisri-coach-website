I also checked key 2026 facts on the web: World Athletics (Sawe 1:59:30 and Kejelcha 1:59:41), Indian Express and HT (Sawan Barwal 2:11:37 and his coach), Olympics.com (Gulveer 13:32.75 and the Sable comeback quote), TOI (Lalita Babar's Mumbai titles) and The Bridge (Markose interview, full text).

Findings: 36 in total (0 high, 20 medium, 16 low). All are in reviews/truth.json.

Top issues:
1. **Sawan Barwal's "Nov–Feb in Ooty" is overstated in 12 places.** The Bridge quotes Markose describing what the programme *normally* does. It does not say Sawan himself spent that winter in the Nilgiris before Rotterdam. HT also says he trained in Colorado Springs.
2. **Sawan's coach is incomplete.** By Nagoya he was following Scott Simmons (Indian Express, HT). The 09b table lists only Markose.
3. **Parul's national records are miscounted.** The book says "six national records" in two places. The count for 2022–26 is seven, and chapter 92 lists all seven.
4. **Lalita Babar's Mumbai wins are misstated.** She did not win "the Mumbai Marathon" three years in a row. She won the Indian women's title in 2012–14.
5. **Smaller unsupported claims:**
   - Ankita is described as "just behind" Parul, but she was 10.6 s back.
   - The EDRP is called "private", but it was run by SAI and Procam.
   - Kavita's 1:12:50 is stated as the record Suriya broke, but records between 2009 and 2017 are unverified.
   - Several chronology and "reported" hedges are missing (the Rawat quote timing, the Lakshmanan DQ "minutes later", Shivnath's 11th place and exact date).

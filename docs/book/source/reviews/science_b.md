## Top issues
1. Backmatter ref: PMID 31191102 is credited to "Benjamin CL". The real first author is Rahimi GRM.
2. Heat-illness signs (Ch. 81 and Ch. 74 stop rules): "stopped sweating / hot, dry skin" is unreliable because heatstroke casualties often still sweat. There is also no advice on cold-water immersion.
3. WBGT >28 °C is labelled "consider postponing". ACSM treats it as the cancel (black-flag) level.
4. Case C: body-mass loss of 1.6–1.8% in a 30-min test does not fit a sweat rate of 1.1–1.3 L/h.
5. Ch. 95: the COACH box has the coach order ferritin and Hb, which conflicts with the book's clinician-first rule. The anaemia survey cited also found that iron deficiency explained less than a third of anaemia.

Assumptions used (please confirm before ordering):
  • Storey heights are not shown anywhere in the drawing set. Assumed: footing top → plinth 8′6″; plinth → GF roof 10′0″; GF roof → FF roof 10′0″ (total column ≈ 28′6″). Stub column C1 = 9′0″. Every 1 ft change in floor height moves column steel by ≈ 45 kg.
  • Built-up area taken as 1,014 sq ft per floor (title block on sheets 02/04/06/08/09/11/12). Sheets 03 and 05 carry a different set of areas (1,709 / 1,599 / 551 = 3,859 sf) — please confirm which is correct.
  • Beam runs scaled off the grid (1–2 = 4′11″, 2–3 = 11′0″, 3–4 = 11′0″; E–D = 7′9″, D–B = 18′1½″, B–A = 12′9″).
  • Slab steel from legend: main Y10 @ 7″ c/c, distribution Y8 @ 6″ c/c, extra top Y10 @ 8″ c/c over 30% of area.
  • Laps at 57 d per drawing note 5; one lap per 12 m stock joint. Hooks 12 d, stirrup hooks 10 d.
  • Cutting wastage 3%. Rod length 12 m (ARS standard). Unit weight d²/162 kg/m.
